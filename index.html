HISTORY OF MARY BEAUTY SALON.

What a special opportunity to have visited MARY BEAUTY SALON!!!. This amazing beauty salon has been existing since the year 2022. The owner of this organisation , MRS FERDINAND MARY, was inspired to create such a beautiful salon at the age of 18. She was relly eager to learn the work as she has been aspiring for fashion at a very tender age. Mrs Mary started learning at a shop that was near where she lived. She became a master of her own after learning for eight months. Mrs Mary stated her business in one of the shops of Roseline Complex at Computer Village, Ikeja. Many apprentice began to troop in and she was overloaded with customers and from there she moved to a better shop which was able to accomodate more customers than the first shop she got.
